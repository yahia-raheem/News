Muhammad Alaa El-Din identified the core issue as the difficulty in harmonizing the water demands of the farm with those of the fish. He elaborated, "Discrepancies arose between irrigation needs and pond drainage capacity, especially during periods of fish hibernation or elevated ammonia levels. The necessity for immediate water drainage due to ammonia buildup often coincided with periods when the land did not require irrigation, leading to unproductive water disposal. Consequently, the experiment was deemed impractical and discontinued."
