Saltwater Reclamation

In December 2015 4 , the government announced the commencement of the 1.5 Million Feddan Project, a key agricultural development initiative within the government's economic reform agenda for the preceding decade. This project seeks to enhance food security, establish sustainable urban communities, and generate employment opportunities for young people through the allocation of desert lands for agricultural reclamation.

The project's implementation was structured in three phases, encompassing nine principal areas within the Western Desert. Among these is the Al-Maghra Oasis, situated in a natural depression spanning approximately 1,600 square kilometers. The oasis's topography, ranging from 50 meters above sea level to 50 meters below sea level, presents a distinctive yet delicate geological context. Its location is approximately 70 kilometers from El Alamein and 300 kilometers from Alexandria, with connectivity to these urban centers facilitated by several unpaved roadways.
