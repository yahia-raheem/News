Law No. 143 of 1981, pertaining to desert lands, applies to the Al-Maghra lands. This law designates the General Authority for Reconstruction Projects and Agricultural Development as the governing body responsible for desert lands allocated for reclamation and cultivation. It is important to note that the Authority, along with the New Urban Communities Authority and the Ministry of Finance, are government shareholders in the NECDC, the project's primary developer. The company's capital is eight billion Egyptian pounds.

Established by Cabinet Resolution No. (6) on November 4, 2015, the NECDC is an Egyptian joint-stock company formed to spearhead the 1.5 million-acre reclamation and development project launched in December 2015. Its board comprises three sitting ministers and six seasoned members. The NECDC is mandated to allocate lands designated for agricultural reclamation to beneficiaries, encompassing both small-scale and large-scale investors, within the project's four million-acre scope.
