The Baghi family's property overlooks Lake Siwa, locally known as "Al-Maraqi," one of four lakes resulting from the accumulation of agricultural drainage water in the oasis since the 1970s. This accumulation stems from the expansion of agricultural reclamation projects on the oasis's periphery, undertaken by both individual investors and small-scale enterprises owning limited land areas, as well as large corporations that have reclaimed over 30,000 acres.
