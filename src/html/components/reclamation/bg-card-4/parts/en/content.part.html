In 2014, Egypt kicked off the “1.5 Million Feddan” project—the start of its fourth phase. This big push covers land reclamation, building new agricultural communities, and putting together related industrial zones. To make it happen, the government set up the New Egyptian Countryside Development Company (NECDC) with eight billion Egyptian pounds in capital. This company took charge of handing out land and overseeing the whole reclamation process. Really, it’s the most recent institutional advancement in Egypt's agricultural development strategy.
