This complex water situation serves as an introduction to the subsequent sections of the "Green Paradise" project. These sections will examine the challenges confronting farmers and analyze the effects of land reclamation on soil quality, salinity levels, and agricultural productivity across three distinct areas, each representing a unique aspect of the crisis. Specifically, we will explore how farmers in Siwa are adapting to the evolving nature of their traditional water resources. In Al-Maghra, we will investigate the recurring setbacks experienced by small investors due to increasing salinity. Finally, in Fayoum, we will focus on the disproportionate impact of agricultural decline on women within their communities.
