Analysis of 2024 data reveals that the Toshka Agricultural Project accounted for approximately 88% of the total area cultivated with alfalfa. In contrast, silage maize cultivation was almost entirely concentrated within the Salhiya Agricultural Project. A five-year trend analysis indicates that water-intensive crops, including alfalfa, sugar beets, and sugarcane, made up approximately 20% of the total cultivated area in 2023, with the majority of this area located within the Toshka Project.
