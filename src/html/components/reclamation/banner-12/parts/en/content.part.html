Agricultural researcher Saqr Al-Nour states, "Growing water-intensive crops like alfalfa and watermelon in arid environments contributes to the accelerated depletion of aquifers and increased soil salinization."

He clarifies that while cultivation of certain crops is technically restricted in reclaimed areas, the enforcement of these regulations appears inconsistent across different areas. He further notes the existence of official lists specifying crops unsuitable for desert land cultivation, including alfalfa, bananas, and rice; however, adherence to these guidelines is often lacking. Disciplinary actions, he suggests, disproportionately affect small-scale farmers, while larger investment initiatives may receive preferential treatment and exemption from these regulations. He points out that some of what these projects produce—especially products like potatoes, citrus fruits, and grapes—ends up getting exported. But honestly, it’s tough to keep track of exactly how much goes out, mostly because there’s just not enough solid data on how these crops are coded or recorded.
