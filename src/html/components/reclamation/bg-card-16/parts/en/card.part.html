Cultivation data from these projects reveals a diverse range of agricultural products. Wheat makes up roughly 16.7% of the total cultivated land, while oranges and alfalfa each account for approximately 11%. The balance of production is distributed across over 60 other crops, including peanuts, silage corn, and high-yield forage varieties.

The crop composition indicates that a considerable share of the total output is derived from high-yield forage crops, including alfalfa and silage corn. This reliance on high-yield crops accounts for the elevated production volume observed despite the constrained cultivated area.
