Who led Egypt’s land-reclamation efforts between 1952 and 2006?

Following decades of land reclamation projects managed by public bodies, a notable shift in responsibilities occurred with the introduction of new, independent economic entities that are not linked to the Authority. Consequently, the Authority's involvement diminished to a marginal 0.26% of the total reclaimed area in 2024.

The NECDC, established as a joint-stock company under Presidential Decree No. 341 of 2016, led land reclamation efforts, accounting for approximately 62.5% of the total area reclaimed during the year. The National Service Projects Organization followed, contributing nearly 20%, with operations primarily focused in the Western Desert and Central Sinai regions. Private sector entities, including local and Arab agricultural investment firms, represented 15.4% of reclaimed land. Agricultural cooperatives and the land reclamation sector of the Ministry of Agriculture accounted for the remaining portion.
