Data from the Central Agency for Public Mobilization and Statistics indicates that the total land area reclaimed from the July Revolution through 2024 is approximately 7.4 million feddans. Notably, the period between 2016 and 2024, coinciding with President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi's administration, represents 32.6% of the total reclaimed area.

In 2024, agricultural land reclamation in Egypt reached a historic peak

The timeframe from 2001 to 2014, during Mubarak’s rule and later when the Supreme Council of the Armed Forces took over, ranks second. It saw a significant increase in reclamation initiatives in the Western Desert and North Sinai, which represented approximately 24.91% of the overall reclaimed land.

Furthermore, the period from 1952 to 1968 ranks third, accounting for 17.20% of the total development, with a notable concentration in regions such as Nubaria. Lastly, the timeframe from 1969 to 1984, during Sadat's rule and the initial phase of Mubarak's presidency, was distinguished by a relatively modest number of projects in contrast to the later waves of development.
