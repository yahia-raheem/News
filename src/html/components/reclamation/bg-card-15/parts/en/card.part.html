Crop Basket

The Central Agency for Public Mobilization and Statistics reported agricultural production data for the 2023/2024 period, indicating that private sector land reclamation initiatives yielded substantial results. Cultivated areas within these projects have reached approximately 72,419 feddans, generating a total production volume of 13.6 million tons. The estimated value of this output is EGP 2.76 billion, representing the aggregate production from land reclamation companies throughout the year.
