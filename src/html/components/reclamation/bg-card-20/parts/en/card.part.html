The study concludes that agricultural expansion in arid regions is occurring without adequate understanding. Current planning frameworks inadequately address the finite nature of water resources, the effects of climate change, and the selection of appropriate crops and irrigation techniques. Recommendations include the establishment of a national groundwater monitoring network with open data access for researchers and policymakers, the development of precise basin-level hydrological models, and the formulation of a long-term management strategy for the unavoidable depletion of aquifers. This strategy should incorporate a clearly defined vision for the future of agricultural communities reliant on these resources.
