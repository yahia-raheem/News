Analysis of project distribution from 2018 to 2024 indicates the consistent prominence of Toshka across multiple years. Notably, in 2022, Toshka represented 82.01% of the total allocated area and led the projects in 2021 with a 60% share. Conversely, Gogreen accounted for the largest proportion of allocated area in 2020, with 27.15%. Prior to this, Toshka held the leading position in 2019, comprising 86% of the total area allocated within this project group.
