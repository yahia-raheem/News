During the specified timeframe, private sector firms initiated agricultural projects covering about 523,000 feddans. Of this, only 240,000 feddans had been fully leveled—removing elevated terrain and subsequent land grading in preparation for infrastructure development (roads, irrigation, and drainage)—according to data from the Central Agency for Public Mobilization and Statistics. The implementation cost so far amounts to approximately EGP 6.8 billion.

Private companies have launched about 50 agricultural reclamation projects across different desert areas listed in the database. In 2024, the Toshka project took the top spot, covering 53.35% of the total reclaimed land. Right behind it, the Gogreen project claimed 30.45%. Gogreen really stands out as a major player among private agricultural investors in the area.
