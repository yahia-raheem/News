The increasing prominence of the NECDC and the National Service Projects Organization suggests a shift in governmental strategies. The NECDC, established in 2016, progressively increased its involvement to encompass 62.5% of all land reclamation activities by 2024. Concurrently, the National Service Projects Organization's participation peaked at 44.8% in 2023, subsequently stabilizing at 20.3% in 2024. These developments suggest that the diminished role of the private sector and cooperatives is not necessarily a permanent trend. Instead, it reflects a deliberate reallocation of state-sponsored projects to specific organizations in recent years. Consequently, forecasting future percentage distributions is of limited value, given the ongoing changes in policies and implementing bodies.
