Land Reclamation Implementers

An analysis of land reclamation implementing agencies from 2014 to 2024 indicates that project distribution has experienced significant shifts, influenced by evolving governmental policies concerning land allocation and project oversight. The private sector's contribution demonstrates considerable volatility, ranging from 21% in 2014 to 66% in 2015. Peak contributions exceeding 80% were observed in select years, such as 2021. However, in 2024, private sector involvement decreased to 15.4% following an expansion of governmental outsourcing to alternative entities.
