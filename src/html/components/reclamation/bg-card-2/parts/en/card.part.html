Subsequent to the July 23, 1952 revolution, the Egyptian government initiated an agricultural expansion program focused on desert reclamation and the establishment of new settlements, implemented across four distinct phases. The initial phase, known as Al-Tahrir Directorate Project, represented the first officially sanctioned land reclamation initiative under President Gamal Abdel Nasser. This project sought to reclaim approximately 600,000 acres to the west of the Nile Delta, with the objective of developing large-scale, state-operated agricultural enterprises. These enterprises aimed to generate employment opportunities, reduce strain on existing agricultural resources, and foster the development of contemporary rural communities.
