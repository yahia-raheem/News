Due to the oasis's distinctive geographical setting and ongoing water drainage issues, government initiatives have further intensified the crisis. As part of the 1.5 Million Feddan Project, the New Egyptian Rural Development Company (NERD), a government entity, offered approximately 30,000 feddans east of the Siwa Oasis for reclamation by companies and beneficiaries in 2018. This offering constituted the second phase of the project, encompassing nine areas across the Western Desert, totaling 490,000 feddans irrigated by groundwater. Despite the government's awareness of the existing agricultural drainage problem affecting the ancient oasis, these lands were included in the reclamation initiative.
