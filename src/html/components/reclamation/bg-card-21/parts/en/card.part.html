He further asserts that the water metering requirements for investors are merely regulatory stipulations. In the absence of a robust system for measuring water extraction, levying water fees, or monitoring daily consumption, the capacity to extract water is determined by the ability to drill deeper wells and incur greater expenses. This, he argues, engenders a distinct disparity in water access, wherein large-scale investors maintain unrestricted cultivation practices, while smallholder farmers are pushed out of the market at the slightest decline in water levels.
