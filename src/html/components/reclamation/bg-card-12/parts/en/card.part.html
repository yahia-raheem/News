Prior to 2014, agricultural land reclamation exhibited a markedly different trend than it does at present. From 2007 to 2013, agricultural cooperative societies were the primary drivers of reclamation projects. Notably, in 2007, these societies accounted for approximately 97% of all reclaimed land, while the private sector contributed only 3%. This disparity diminished over time, and by 2013, the private sector's involvement had grown substantially, approaching the scale of cooperative initiatives. In that year, cooperatives managed 52.6% of reclamation efforts, compared to the private sector's 45%. The General Authority for Reconstruction Projects and Agricultural Development maintained a consistently minor role throughout this period, with its cumulative share remaining below 2-3% of total reclamation activities.
