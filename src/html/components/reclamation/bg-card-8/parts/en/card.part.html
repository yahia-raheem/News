Based on the database we've assembled, the administrative oversight of agricultural reclamation initiatives experienced a series of structural modifications following the July 23 Revolution. From the 1950s through the mid-1970s, numerous agencies oversaw the early projects, starting with the Permanent Authority for Land Reclamation, which merged with the Al-Tahrir Directorate in 1957. This evolved through several iterations of institutions and bodies, ultimately leading to the establishment of the General Authority for Reconstruction and Agricultural Development Projects in 1975. This entity took over the responsibilities of the earlier entities, including the Executive Authority for Desert Projects.
