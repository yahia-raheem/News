During the 1970s and 80s, the government tried to get more people involved in land reclamation. One big effort was the “Youth Graduates” program, which started in 1987. The idea was pretty simple—to help recent grads and folks out of work get their own small plots of land in new areas. Things started shifting in the mid-90s. Instead of just focusing on small plots, the government pushed for much bigger projects and drew in both local and international investors. That’s when projects like Toshka, El Salam, and El Oweinat took off. Most of these were way out in the desert, far from the crowded Nile Valley and Delta.
