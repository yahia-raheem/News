The research underscores salinity as a crucial determinant of irrigation water quality. Electrical conductivity (EC) values in most samples surpassed the permissible threshold for irrigation (2250 microsiemens/cm), indicating that the high salinity levels pose a threat to most crops, excluding salt-tolerant varieties.

Issa shared the land with his five sisters, but when it became tainted by salinity, he had no choice but to leave it behind and pursue a different career: plastering and construction. He is now involved in building and painting homes and factories. He expresses, "I require income to support my family, and the land can no longer deliver that." His sisters, who once thrived by harvesting olives and dates for sale or personal use, now find themselves, after the loss of their husbands, depending on basic manual work within their homes to care for their children.
