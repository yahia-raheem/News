Geographic Information System (GIS) data and satellite imagery analysis reveal that the area of Lake Siwa alone increased by 21% between 1975 and 2025, while the combined area of the drainage lakes expanded by 35.12% in just five years.

In the second phase of the project (…….), utilizing geographic information systems (GIS) techniques and testimonies from oasis residents, we document the ecological changes that the historic oasis has experienced over the last fifty years (1975–2025). This includes the inundation and salinization of sections of the land traditionally held by the indigenous community, compelling them to leave in pursuit of alternative sources of income. Furthermore, we highlight how the rising number of wells drilled to support agricultural reclamation initiatives and export-oriented crops has intensified the agricultural drainage crisis.
