An Ongoing Crisis

With the assistance of a research team specializing in geographic information systems (GIS) and satellite imagery, we analyzed six atellite images, captured between 1975 and 2025 at ten-year intervals, which demonstrated a significant expansion of water bodies in Siwa. Drainage and salt lakes have collectively increased by 116.04%. Specifically, the area encompassed by the four drainage lakes has grown by 35.12% during the observed period, while salt lakes have expanded by 322%.
