Shwayrif has moved away from the agricultural roots of his forefathers, focusing instead on the tourism sector. He runs a quaint shop in the heart of Siwa and also works as a chef in a local restaurant to provide for his family. Nevertheless, he still feels a strong connection to his land, viewing it as a family legacy that he must preserve and hopes to pass it on to his four children, despite the daily challenges posed by water scarcity.
