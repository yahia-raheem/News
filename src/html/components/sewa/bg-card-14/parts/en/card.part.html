A review of the administrative divisions within the Siwa Oasis reveals a marked decline in traditional agricultural practices in several sheikhdoms/villages. Specifically, Umm al-Saghir and al-Maraqi have experienced a near-total loss of cultivated land. This trend is further exemplified by Bahi al-Din, where cultivated land diminished from 2.46 square kilometers to a mere 0.04 square kilometers, representing a 98.4% reduction.

Agurmi witnessed a substantial contraction in traditional agricultural area, decreasing from 3.08 square kilometers to 0.60 square kilometers, an 80.5% decrease. Abu Sharouf-al-Kafrawi also experienced a significant decline, with cultivated land decreasing from 14.23 square kilometers to 6.81 square kilometers, a 52.1% decrease. Siwa City demonstrated the most resilience, yet still experienced a 20% reduction, with cultivated land decreasing from 8.98 square kilometers to 7.18 square kilometers.
