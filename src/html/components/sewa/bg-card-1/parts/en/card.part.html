Abu Bakr Baghi inherited 18 acres in Siwa, a family property for over a century, renowned for producing premium dates and olives. Approximately 50 years ago, however, Baghi observed the encroachment of a lake, formed by agricultural drainage from newly reclaimed land, onto his historic acreage. This resulted in the loss of approximately 10 acres and left the remaining eight severely affected by salinity, which decimated crop yields.
