Haider began working the land at the age of six, collecting fallen dates to feed his livestock. Now, forty years later, he still humbly identifies himself as "a farmer from Siwa." His story, from those early days to his current responsibilities, reflects a multi-generational commitment to farming in Siwa, a legacy passed down from father to son, as he shared in our interview.

Haider's relationship with the land deepened with experience and increasing responsibilities. Following his military service, he dedicated himself entirely to his farm. Farming became his sole profession, as he hired workers and shared the labor.
