Sparkling Wells

Amr Baghi identifies the uncontrolled nature of artesian wells, locally known as "sparkling wells," as a contributing factor to the waterlogging issue. These wells flow naturally, and both investors and farmers in Siwa are reluctant to seal them, Baghi shared with the reporter.

Baghi elaborates, “Siwa's investors and farmers traditionally avoid closing these wells due to a belief that it diminishes their flow over time. Consequently, many wells remain continuously open. While some farmers utilize these wells for temporary water storage during off-planting hours, many others allow excess irrigation water to drain away, leading to continuous operation of numerous artesian wells. If we consider that just ten farmers engage in this practice, that results in ten sparkling wells running non-stop, 24 hours a day, which inevitably causes a substantial increase in the water level."
