Khater attributes his agricultural challenges to increased water salinity. Khater's main issue is the water's salinity. In his discussion with the reporter, he mentioned that they originally depended on water sourced from older wells, until a government directive was issued to drill new wells (a recurring action we have recorded in the news archives).

He explains, "These wells initially provided adequate water, but after ten to fifteen years, the water became brackish and saline, leading to the complete decline of agriculture. Consequently, no crops have grown on this land for fifteen years. The orchards, once our primary source of income and painstakingly cultivated by our forefathers, have withered. We inherited this legacy but were unable to sustain it. The water's degradation ruined everything, and farming as they once did became impossible."
