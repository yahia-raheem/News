A 2023 study, co-authored by a team of researchers, investigated groundwater quality in the Siwa Oasis, providing a scientific analysis of the impact of new well drilling on the oasis's water resources. The study analyzed 106 water samples collected in January 2022, processed at the Desert Research Center laboratory of the Ministry of Agriculture and Land Reclamation according to established standard procedures. Results indicated that groundwater salinization is primarily attributable to dissolution and ion exchange processes, with calcium, chloride, magnesium, sulfate, and sodium ions identified as the most significant contributors to increased salinity levels.
