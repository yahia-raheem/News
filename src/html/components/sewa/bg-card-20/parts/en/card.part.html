Increasing Salinity

Knooz Siwa, an NECDC subsidiary involved in the new project, cultivates both water-intensive field crops (wheat, barley, corn, and fava beans) and traditional crops (palm and olive trees) in the Siwa Oasis. This is facilitated by a low-salinity (200 ppm) well, 1,050 meters deep, powered by solar energy, and yielding approximately 500 cubic meters per hour. However, local farmer Issa Khater, a 35-year-old Siwan, struggles to irrigate his inherited land in central Siwa due to increasing water salinity.
