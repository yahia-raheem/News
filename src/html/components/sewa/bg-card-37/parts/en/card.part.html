Haider described the government's attempts to address the water crisis in Siwa: "Two decades ago, the state began drilling compensatory wells to a depth of 100–150 meters, and later, deep wells, employing a 1:9 freshwater-salt water mixing system to improve irrigation." Haider has observed an improvement in water quality on his land in the last three or four years, but he views this as merely the beginning of an ongoing process.

He also believes that the issue of water access is not a conflict between "residents" and "investors," but rather a matter of equitable rationing and responsible water management that applies to everyone: "We must recognize the value of water per unit and rationalize our withdrawals, regardless of whether we are Siwans or investors," Haider explained.
