Sixty Years of Reclamation:

In 1954, the Egyptian government formally initiated its desert reclamation program with the enactment of Law No. 169, which established the Permanent Authority for Land Reclamation. Subsequent legislation and decrees consolidated and reorganized the institutions responsible for desert development. This culminated in Decree No. 269 of 1975, which established the General Authority for Reconstruction Projects and Agricultural Development. This Authority subsequently served as the primary administrative body for desert land management for several decades.
